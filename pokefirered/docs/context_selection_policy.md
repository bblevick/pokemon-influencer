# Public context selection v1

`config/context_selection_v1.json` defines **campaign-public-context-1** for new scripted encounter opportunities. Explicit content adoption binds its hash; the accepted Save20 campaign has not adopted it. This is a context relevance policy, not a replacement diffusion policy. Existing publication epochs, recipient exposures, memos, commitments and checkpoints retain their original versions.

Durable actor knowledge and opportunity context are separate. `strategy.selection.public_candidates` enumerates actor-granted delivery receipts, filters the `pokebook` channel before looking up a source descriptor, and verifies publication, claim, sender and existing exposure. It never delivers information or deletes memory. Private `ROCKET_INTEL`, private director memory and unexposed public posts cannot enter this selection.

Cerulean is source-bound map3/3 at progression order8, following Route4 order7. Source encounters332–334 bind to Cerulean regardless of the persistent Rival's Pallet identity/home-stage. Public source stages remain their original recorded stages. Campaign distance is the absolute authored stage-order difference, not geometric map distance or elapsed wall time. CURRENT requires distance≤1 and publication lag≤1; RECENT requires distance≤3 and lag≤4; otherwise OLDER. Unqualified origins have unknown distance and remain OLDER. Lag measures recorded publication order, not action frequency.

The deterministic priority is significant public events first, then CURRENT/RECENT/OLDER, independent origins, tactically distinct capabilities, latest publication, and stable claim hash. Source quality is HIGH **reported public evidence**, never promoted to direct observation or guaranteed future opponent state. Older Gym outcomes remain relevant significant history without being relabeled CURRENT.

Capabilities are normalized across encounters by removing battle/trainer occurrence metadata; outcomes retain battle identity. Full receipt IDs, post IDs, immediate and original authors, source stage, publication order and relevance remain in each selected reference. Human labels derive from the pinned catalog. Independent origins use original author plus originating battle; repeated deliveries/reposts from one origin do not count as additional corroboration. Counts never imply move/action frequency. V1 includes at most one full reference per normalized claim in the small prompt; the audit retains **all** references and origin counts. This is a context reference budget, not loss of knowledge.

Conflicting terminal results for the same battle form an atomic group: include all sides with reciprocal conflict IDs or omit the whole group from this opportunity. Different observed species/moves are compatible history, not contradictory future predictions. Contradictions and omitted references remain durable and auditable.

Packing uses complete JSON claim groups; it never slices IDs, prose or multibyte characters. The evidence cap is4608 bytes, full-context target7680, absolute full StrategyContext maximum8192 UTF-8 bytes. Own trainer identity, personality, companions, legal envelope summary, finite resources, unknowns and host scope are preserved first. The resolver reduces the evidence budget in256-byte steps against the **complete serialized packet**, including metadata. If the required packet cannot fit, it fails closed. The chat adapter independently applies its existing24576-byte request limit; no transport limits were increased.

`schemas/strategy/selected_public_context.schema.json` validates the selected view. Historical Strategy schema/configuration hashes remain unchanged. The base context validates under its existing schema; only the explicit scripted-content path applies this internal transformation and validates the new public component.

Jobs store policy ID/version/hash, opportunity and stage, selected receipt IDs, effective byte budget, full model input and context/material hashes. A committed encounter reads the job's exact selected facts; browsing, restart, new posts, model recovery or later policy changes cannot rebuild it. Changing selection semantics requires a future explicit version/content adoption. Fresh opportunities may select a different subset while earlier jobs and all durable knowledge remain intact. No inference runs during selection.
