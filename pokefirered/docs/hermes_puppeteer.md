Always-on is persistent event-driven availability with bounded jobs, not continuous inference or a long-lived unrestricted conversation. Fresh worker homes retrieve branch-aware game memory explicitly; deleting a home never deletes continuity. Canonical events, director summaries, private actor observations and published claims are different authorized views. Personal Hermes MEMORY.md/USER.md/transcripts/plugins never supply NPC knowledge.

PokeBook is the social-memory system and its in-world interface. Game-event-driven services schedule bounded NPC exposure without requiring player feed opening. Publication, unread delivery, actual exposure and receipt-backed retained knowledge remain separate. The existing SOCIAL service provides explicit exposure; automatic scheduling needs separate implementation/acceptance.

Director retrieval may select lawful notes, but it cannot copy omniscient memory into an NPC. Briefings contain only actor-authorized observations/exposed claims or explicitly delivered notes with retained provenance. A new claim must traverse a targeted authorized delivery receipt. Hypotheses remain labeled interpretations, not canonical facts. Strategy later interprets these briefings with personality/own companions/inventory and legal preparation envelope; Hermes does not bypass feasibility or directly edit parties.

The trusted host issues bound actor/campaign/branch/cutoff/epoch grants before retrieval; models cannot select another actor, widen grants, advance history, grant resources or manufacture exposure. Do not expose stores/grant factories/unrestricted SQL/canonical ledgers, terminal/filesystem/browser/emulator access, unrelated plugins or delegation. Current tool whitelist/cleanup limits remain in `hermes_runtime_reset.md`. Any expanded note/briefing tools require a separately reviewed scope and schemas; this document does not activate them.

Provider failures cannot suppress mandatory recaps or erase memory. Stored decisions/receipts replay without inference; restores hide future summaries and revoke stale jobs. Preserve current model slots and normal offline default. No strategic advice, System One control, team changes, full UI, reputation or endgame is authorized by this documentation change.
